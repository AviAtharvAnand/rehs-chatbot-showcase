We have also seen increases of network bandwidth, from mega to Giga, and 100s of Gbps. Despite this high bandwidth capacity, researchers are still not experiencing the corresponding increase in the speed of data transfer. This is due to multiple factors: server, transport protocol, etc.

**Move That Data!** was the first-ever Data Mover challenge on a global scale and the inaugural edition held in Singapore at **[Asia 2019 Supercomputing Conference](https://www.nscc.sg/)**. The challenge aimed to bring together experts from industry and academia, in a bid to test their software across servers located in various countries (USA, Singapore, Australia, Japan) that are connected by 100G international networks.
